The story so far

I like the moment a messy set of numbers turns into something a director can act on.

My career started in Colombo, valuing institutional fund portfolios for HSBC’s Edinburgh-based clients — daily Net Asset Values where every figure had to be right before the cut-off. From there I moved into healthcare revenue-cycle finance for US clients, where I was promoted twice in just over two years and rebuilt how receivables were recorded.

In 2022 I moved to Scotland for an MSc in Accounting & Finance at the University of Dundee. Today I’m the entire in-house finance function for an international chess-publishing group — three companies across the UK and the Netherlands — and the only person who sees all of their numbers at once.

I’m CIMA part-qualified and working towards CGMA, and I’m a member of the GLG expert network, advising on finance and multi-entity accounting.

Moments that define my work

No handover. No interruption.

Took over a foreign ledger overnight

When the Dutch company’s accountant resigned, I absorbed its entire accounting function with no handover period — and reporting, filing and supplier payments carried on without a missed beat.

1 company → 3 companies

Grew the role as the group grew

Started as bookkeeper for one company. As the group expanded through acquisition and incorporation, I took on finance and operations for all three — with no increase in finance headcount.

Trusted reviewer

The last check before filing

I review and challenge the VAT returns and annual accounts prepared by the group’s external firms, agreeing the figures before they go to the directors for sign-off.

Accuracy by design

Rebuilt receivables for auditability

At HealthRecon I introduced a double-entry system for accounts receivable, making the ledger more accurate and far easier to audit — and was twice named a company Value Champion.
